Brand kit

Everyday goodness, thoughtfully presented.

The VT Fresh Chicken identity pairs a simple line-drawn chicken — its wing shaped like a leaf — with a crisp red, white and black palette. The leaf wing is a nod to natural, honest food; it is a design motif, not a certification mark.

This kit contains the logo files, colours, typography guidance and usage rules needed to present the brand consistently.

Colour

Palette

Clean, confident, butcher-counter colours. White is the paper; black is the voice; signature red is the accent — used with intent, never as wallpaper.

White#FFFFFF — primary background
Black#171717 — primary colour, headings
Signature red#C9232C — accent, CTAs, comb, focus
Deep red#A61922 — red hover/active state
Body gray#555555 — body copy, captions
Neutral surface#F6F6F4 — panels, strips
Hairline#E3E3E0 — borders, separators
Pale red#FDECEE — soft highlight fills

Accessible pairings: black on white, white on black, and white on signature red all pass for text. Use red for accents and actions — not for small body text, and not on pale red.

Typography

Type

Headings are set in Fraunces on the website (loaded as --font-fraunces); Georgia is the system fallback and is what the SVG logos embed. Body text uses the system sans stack.

Good chicken. Great meals.

Display — Fraunces / Georgia serif. Large, light, tight letter-spacing (−0.03 to −0.05em). Italic for emphasis words only, in signature red.

From a quick weekday curry to a Sunday family feast.

Body — system sans. 16px minimum, line-height 1.65, in body gray on white.

Motion

Motion

Motion is quiet and purposeful — it should never delay or hide content.

  • One-time entrances only: 600ms fade-and-rise (18px), staggered by at most 120ms.
  • The hero photograph settles once on first load: 900ms, scale 1.04 → 1.
  • No parallax, carousels, marquees or looping animation.
  • With prefers-reduced-motion, everything renders static — no animation at all.

Usage

Clear space & rules

Give the logo room to breathe: clear space equals ¼ of the emblem width on all sides.

Minimum sizes — full logo: 160px digital / 40mm print; emblem alone: 24px digital.

  • Do keep the emblem’s proportions — never stretch, crop or rotate it.
  • Do place the logo on white, neutral, black or photography with enough contrast — never on low-contrast busy backgrounds.
  • Do keep the signature-red comb; it disappears only in the monochrome version.
  • Don’t use the leaf wing to imply organic or any certification — it is a decorative motif.
  • Don’t add claims the business hasn’t verified (free-delivery thresholds, ratings, farm sourcing) to brand materials.
  • Tone: warm, plain and honest — write like a good neighbour, not an advert.
  • Photography: natural light, real meals, real kitchens. No studio gloss.
