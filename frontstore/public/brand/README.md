## Design notes

- Emblem: original line-drawn chicken whose wing is a leaf — a natural-food motif,
  **not** an organic or certification claim. Strokes are black `#171717` with
  rounded caps on a white `#FFFFFF` roundel; the comb is signature red
  `#C9232C`. The reversed version inverts to white strokes on black.
- Colours: white `#FFFFFF`, black `#171717`, signature red `#C9232C`,
  deep red (hover) `#A61922`, body gray `#555555`, neutral surface `#F6F6F4`,
  hairline `#E3E3E0`, pale red `#FDECEE`.
- Type: SVG text remains editable in Georgia; the website uses Fraunces.
  To match the website in print, set the editable wordmark to Fraunces in
  your design tool and outline text for final artwork.
- PNGs have transparent backgrounds. Re-export at other sizes from the SVGs
  (e.g. `qlmanage`, Inkscape, or a browser screenshot) — the SVG is source of truth.
- Clear space: ¼ of emblem width on all sides.
